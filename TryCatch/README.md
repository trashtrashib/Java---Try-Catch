## Cooking Game
This program runs an arcade style game where you pick ingrediants and create (often goofy looking) meals to get points looking for a highscore.

## Ingrediants
Each ingrediant you are offered has 4 statistics: Nutrition, Calories, Size and its food group.

* Nutrition is the value which is converted into points when a meal is completed. Higher nutrition is better.

* Size increases how much stomach space that ingrediant takes up, if the meals total size exceeds how much room you have left in your stomach, it can't be eaten. Lower size is better.

* Food group and calories determine a multiplier that increases the number of points you earn for each point of nutrition in your meal. You receive a multiplier bonus for each food group present in your meal and for having similar calorie amounts to each other. Additionally you lose multiplier for ever 500 calories of a single food group in a single meal.
* For instance if you have 100 calories of fruits and 90 calories of vegatables, you'll receive a multiplier bonus for having two food groups and an additional bonus because two of your food groups have similar calorie counts. However if you have 100 calories of fruits and 200 calories of vegatables, you will receive a bonus to your multiplier for having two foodgroups, but since the number of calories aren't very close, you don't receive additional multiplier.
* You receive multiplier primarily from the current meal (turn) but you receive a lower bonus from the total calorie counts from previous meals as well.

## Preparing Ingrediants

Each turn, you can 'prepare' a limited number of the ingrediants you add, modifying them to potentially fit your meal better. Once an ingrediant has been prepared it cannot be changed back.

## Special Effects

Some ingrediants have special effects that can modify the game. These will be shown with the text 'Special: ' in an ingrediants description. Most effects are a result of preparing an ingrediant, but some ingrediants have abilities regardless.

## Turn Structure

Each turn you are offered 5 ingrediants to choose from, you may prepare ingrediants from this list, and/or add them to the meal. Once an ingrediant has been added, it cannot be removed or prepared. Whenever you add an ingrediant it will be replaced so the number of ingrediants offered is always the same. When you are satisfied with the current meal, you can end the turn, if the meal does not exceed the stomach limit you will receive score equal to the total nutrition value times your multiplier and the size and calories will be added to the round totals. Once a turn ends, the meal and offered ingrediants are reset and you can begin creating another meal.

Each game has a limited number of turns to earn points, when the last turn finishes your final score is determined. While the number of turns is limited, you will probably run out of stomach space before the final turn, so be careful.
